PingMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

PingMe is a WhatsApp-based reminder service that allows users to create and receive reminders through WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

When you use PingMe, we may collect and process information required to provide the reminder service, including:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • The reminder messages and tasks you send to PingMe.
  • The date and time associated with your reminders.
  • Information required to process WhatsApp messages and deliver reminders.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create, schedule, manage, and deliver your reminders through WhatsApp. We may also use technical information to maintain and improve the service.

WhatsApp

PingMe uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to receive and send messages. Messages sent to PingMe are processed to provide the requested reminder functionality.

Data Storage

Reminder information may be stored in our application database for the purpose of providing the service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

We only use third-party services where necessary to operate PingMe, including infrastructure and WhatsApp messaging services. We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by PingMe. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can stop using PingMe at any time. You may also contact us to request information about or deletion of data associated with your use of the service, subject to applicable requirements.
